\begin{abstract}
Most vision-language-action (VLA) policies operate on 2D images, yet
robots act in a metric 3D workspace whose configuration evolves over
time, a 4D problem. Methods that inject 3D or 4D structure often rely on
depth sensors, point-cloud encoders, or heavy scene reconstruction, which
increases model size, training cost, and deployment latency; many of them
still treat spatial geometry and temporal dynamics as separate concerns.
World-model-augmented VLAs predict future 2D video but rarely align that
visual foresight with the robot's own metric body motion, so predicted
actions can be temporally coherent in pixel space yet physically
inconsistent in 3D. We present \textbf{4DW-VLA}, a \textbf{4D and
world-model aligned VLA} whose central component is a dedicated
\textbf{4D Future Expert} that predicts future 7D link trajectories
(3D position + orientation) from forward kinematics (FK) at negligible
cost, orders of magnitude cheaper than point-cloud or depth-pipeline 4D
extraction. Unlike prior FK-based approaches that discard the 4D branch
after training, the 4D Future Expert is \textbf{retained at deployment}
and continues to supply metric body-motion context to the action head.
A compact \textbf{World Knowledge Expert} exchanges information with the
4D expert through \textbf{Unified Bidirectional Cross-Attention (UBCA)},
producing 2D-foresight tokens aligned with predicted 4D motion; these
tokens supervise a \textbf{frozen} large video world model during
training only. At inference, the world model is removed; the VLM, the two
lightweight experts, and the action head remain, yielding a small
deployable policy with no video-generation cost. On three RoboTwin~2.0 bimanual tasks, 4DW-VLA
matches or exceeds representative baselines from the \textbf{2D-foresight}
and \textbf{depth-plus-video} VLA families, with the largest gains under
domain randomization.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\IEEEPARstart{L}{earning-based} manipulation policies and VLA
models~\cite{rt1,rt2,chi2023diffusion,zhao2023act,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0}
have improved language following and cross-task generalization, but they
still under-represent the physical world in which a robot moves.

\textbf{Most VLAs are fundamentally 2D.} They consume RGB images and
language, yet manipulation unfolds in a 3D workspace; when time is
included, the relevant object is \textbf{4D} structure: how 3D geometry
changes across the action horizon. A policy that reasons only in image
space lacks explicit metric body motion and cannot guarantee that
predicted actions respect the robot's own spatial evolution.

\textbf{3D-/4D-aware VLAs often pay a heavy geometric tax.} Point clouds,
depth maps, voxel grids, and 3D Gaussian fields can improve spatial
reasoning~\cite{ze2024dp3,yang2025fp3,gervet2023act3d}, but they typically
require extra sensors or large perception networks to extract and encode
geometry at every timestep. The resulting models are bigger, slower to
train, and harder to deploy. Moreover, many 3D-enhanced designs treat
geometry as a \textbf{static} per-frame cue and under-model
\textbf{temporal} changes in the robot's own configuration.

\textbf{Temporal prediction is usually misaligned across modalities.}
Actions are inherently future-oriented: a policy outputs action chunks
over horizon~$H$. Yet most VLAs do not jointly predict how \textbf{2D
observations} and \textbf{3D/4D body state} should co-evolve over that
horizon. World-model and world-action-model (WAM) lines predict future
video~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,nvidia2026wam,lawam2026}, but often without
explicit metric 4D body motion; the camera may ``imagine'' a future that
is not consistent with the robot's actual 3D trajectory (e.g., the arm
rises in joint space but the predicted video does not show the raised
hand).

\textbf{These signals should be coupled.} When a robot lifts its
end-effector in 3D, the egocentric camera stream should reflect that
motion; conversely, visual foresight should not contradict feasible body
dynamics. Effective manipulation therefore requires \textbf{aligning}
(a)~future \textbf{4D body motion} in the robot frame and (b)~future
\textbf{2D video} the camera will see, and using both to predict
(c)~future \textbf{actions}.

We propose \textbf{4DW-VLA}, a \textbf{4D and World-model aligned VLA},
to address the above gaps. Our design rests on three ideas:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A dedicated 4D Future Expert, retained at deployment.}
  Instead of reconstructing scene geometry from depth or point clouds, we
  compute 7D link trajectories (3D position + orientation) directly from
  proprioception via FK, at orders-of-magnitude lower cost than
  perception-based 4D pipelines. Unlike prior FK-based approaches that
  treat 4D as a training-time auxiliary discarded at inference, the 4D
  Future Expert is a first-class attention path that \textbf{remains
  active at deployment}, continuously supplying metric body-motion
  structure to the action head.
\item \textbf{4D--2D alignment via bidirectional cross-attention.} The 4D
  Future Expert and a compact \textbf{World Knowledge Expert} exchange
  information through \textbf{Unified Bidirectional Cross-Attention
  (UBCA)}: at every timestep, each expert attends causally within its own
  stream and to past tokens of the other, coupling 4D body foresight and
  2D visual foresight before action decoding.
\item \textbf{Train with a large frozen world model; deploy without it.}
  The World Knowledge Expert produces compact \textbf{implicit
  2D-foresight tokens} already aligned with 4D predictions. During
  training, these tokens condition a \textbf{frozen} large video diffusion
  world model; at inference the world model is \textbf{discarded}, so the
  deployable VLA stays small and fast while retaining distilled,
  4D-aligned visual dynamics.
\end{enumerate}

Fig.~\ref{fig:overview} summarizes the architecture. We co-train four
objectives (current 3D state estimation, future 4D prediction, future 2D
foresight distillation, and action-chunk generation) under one weighted
multi-task loss (Section~\ref{sec:training}). The video world model
$g_{\mathrm{wm}}$ stays \textbf{frozen}; the VLM backbone is fine-tuned
at \textbf{one-fifth} the learning rate of the expert and action heads
over \textbf{10{,}000} optimizer steps.

\textbf{Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item We introduce a \textbf{4D Future Expert} that uses
  \textbf{FK-derived 7D link trajectories} as a lightweight body-centric
  4D representation and is \textbf{retained at deployment}, unlike prior
  FK-based approaches that discard the 4D branch after training
  (Section~\ref{sec:4d}).
\item We couple the 4D Future Expert with a compact \textbf{World
  Knowledge Expert} through \textbf{UBCA}, aligning future metric body
  trajectories with future egocentric video to jointly condition chunked
  action prediction (Section~\ref{sec:wk}).
\item We distill a \textbf{frozen} video world model into trainable
  foresight tokens and \textbf{remove} it at inference, preserving
  4D--2D alignment without deployment-time video generation
  (Sections~\ref{sec:wk}--\ref{sec:inference}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Foundation Models in Robotics}

Generalist robot policies are trained on large multi-task
datasets~\cite{walke2023bridge,khazatsky2024droid,vuong2023oxe} and
increasingly built on vision-language
priors~\cite{rt2,kim2024openvla,black2024pi0,liu2024rdt}. RT-2~\cite{rt2}
and OpenVLA~\cite{kim2024openvla} cast actions as language-model tokens;
Diffusion Policy~\cite{chi2023diffusion}, RDT~\cite{liu2024rdt}, and
$\pi_0$~\cite{black2024pi0} generate continuous action chunks via
diffusion or flow matching. These foundations excel at semantic
generalization but largely treat observations as 2D images without
explicit 4D body--video alignment.

World-action models (WAMs) co-train video prediction with
control~\cite{nvidia2026wam,lawam2026,dreaming2026}: the predicted future
regularizes the action pathway, and generation is often dropped at test
time. \textbf{4DW-VLA} follows the ``train with foresight, infer without
the generator'' principle, but differs in \textbf{what} is aligned: we
explicitly couple \textbf{FK-derived 4D body trajectories} with
\textbf{2D video foresight} through bidirectional cross-attention before
action decoding, rather than relying on video prediction alone.

\subsection{3D and 4D Representations for Manipulation}

A growing line injects 3D or 4D structure into manipulation policies and
VLAs. These methods improve spatial and temporal reasoning, but the
\textbf{4D signals they use are typically heavy}: they require extra
sensing, large encoders, auxiliary foundation models, or scene-scale
reconstruction, which inflates compute, memory, and model size.
\textbf{4DW-VLA} deliberately avoids this tax by deriving 4D body motion
from FK and distilling 2D dynamics into a small expert rather than
embedding a full world model in the deployable policy.

\textbf{RynnWorld-4D}~\cite{rynnworld4d2026} builds a tri-branch
diffusion world model that co-generates future RGB, \textbf{depth}, and
\textbf{optical flow} from RGB-D input, curates hundreds of millions of
frames with pseudo depth/flow labels, and trains an inverse-dynamics
policy on internal 4D latents, an \textbf{infrastructure-heavy} approach.
\textbf{4D-VLA}~\cite{fourdvla2025} injects sequential \textbf{RGB-D}
frames with 3D coordinate embeddings into VLA pretraining; \textbf{depth
must be available or estimated for every frame}.
\textbf{Pri4R}~\cite{pri4r2026} supervises VLMs with \textbf{privileged
scene point tracks} from depth-based tracking, \textbf{expensive to
obtain and encode}. \textbf{Lift3D-VLA}~\cite{lift3dvla2026} lifts VLAs
with explicit \textbf{point clouds}, GC-MAE pretraining over reconstructed
geometry, and layer-wise temporal action modeling, adding
\textbf{substantial parameters and preprocessing}.
\textbf{SpatialVLA}~\cite{spatialvla2025} integrates \textbf{monocular
depth estimation} into a 4B-scale VLA, introducing a \textbf{permanent
depth branch} at inference unless distilled away.
\textbf{4D-WAM}~\cite{fourdwam2026} aligns WAMs with dense
\textbf{pixel-level 3D trajectory fields} from a large 4D foundation
teacher.

In contrast, \textbf{4DW-VLA} obtains 4D through \textbf{FK on
proprioception} (microseconds on CPU, no depth network, no point cloud)
and aligns it to 2D foresight through \textbf{two small coupled experts},
using a frozen world model only as a \textbf{training-time teacher}. The
deployable model excludes the teacher entirely.

\subsection{World Models and World-Action Models}

Classical world models predict future states for planning or
representation
learning~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,levy2026simdist,hansen2024tdmpc2}.
Recent WAMs make the future
\textbf{action-facing}~\cite{nvidia2026wam,wamsurvey2026}: video or
latent subgoals regularize the
policy~\cite{lawam2026,dreaming2026}. \textbf{4DW-VLA} contributes a
complementary axis: the training signal is not only ``predict future
pixels'' but \textbf{align future pixels with future FK 4D body motion}
via bidirectional cross-attention, then condition actions on both
distilled pathways. The large video model is frozen and removed at
deployment, similar in spirit to fast WAM recipes but with explicit
\textbf{4D--2D coupling}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

We present 4DW-VLA, a 4D and world-model aligned VLA for
language-conditioned manipulation. A VLM encodes multi-view RGB and
instructions; three trainable heads (a \textbf{4D Future Expert}, a
\textbf{World Knowledge Expert}, and an \textbf{Action Expert}) cooperate
through UBCA. Fig.~\ref{fig:overview} shows the overview.

\begin{figure*}[!t]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{robot_architecture_illustrator_safe_v3_1.png}
    \caption{\textbf{4DW-VLA architecture.} Robot state $q_t$ and
    FK-derived 4D trajectories are patchified, tokenized, and fed into
    the \textbf{4D Future Expert}, whose 4D Encoder performs self-attention
    and cross-attention to the VLM prefix. A shared output head predicts
    both current and future 7D keypoints ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{4D}}$).
    The resulting tokens $Z^{\mathrm{4D}}$ are exchanged with the
    \textbf{World Knowledge Expert} through \textbf{UBCA}, producing
    implicit 2D-foresight tokens $Z^{\mathrm{wk}}$ that supervise a
    \textbf{frozen} video world model during training only. The
    \textbf{Action Expert} attends to the VLM, both expert outputs, and
    generates action chunks ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{act}}$). At inference
    the world model is dropped.}
    \label{fig:overview}
\end{figure*}

\subsection{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

Let $o_t$ denote multi-view RGB, $\ell_t$ the language instruction, and
$q_t$ proprioception. Write $p_t$ for the current metric 3D body state
(FK-derived link poses), $P_{t+1:t+H}$ for the future 4D body
trajectory, and $V_{t+1:t+T}$ for future egocentric video.

\textbf{4D--2D future alignment:}
\begin{equation}
p\bigl(P_{t+1:t+H},\, V_{t+1:t+T} \mid o_t,\, \ell_t,\, q_t\bigr).
\label{eq:align}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Action prediction:}
\begin{multline}
p\bigl(A_t \mid P_{t+1:t+H},\, V_{t+1:t+T},\, o_t,\, \ell_t,\, q_t\bigr),\\
A_t = [a_t, \ldots, a_{t+H-1}].
\label{eq:action}
\end{multline}

At deployment we use compact representations $Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t$ and
$Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t$:
\begin{equation}
p\bigl(A_t \mid Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t,\, Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t,\, o_t,\, \ell_t,\, q_t\bigr).
\label{eq:action-deploy}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Current 3D state estimation} (auxiliary):
\begin{equation}
p\bigl(p_t \mid o_t,\, \ell_t,\, P^{\mathrm{hist}}_t\bigr),
\label{eq:cur}
\end{equation}
where $P^{\mathrm{hist}}_t$ is a short history of FK 4D tracks.

\subsection{4D Future Expert}
\label{sec:4d}

\textbf{Motivation.} Point-cloud, depth, and scene-track 4D
representations require sensing or heavy networks
(Section~\ref{sec:related}). Manipulators already expose $q_t$; FK maps
joints to link poses in microseconds. We therefore build 4D body
supervision from \textbf{FK only}, gaining orders-of-magnitude lower
latency and zero extra sensors compared with perception-based 4D
pipelines.

\textbf{7D link trajectories.} For each link/keypoint
$k \in \{1,\ldots,K\}$,
\begin{equation}
x^k_t = \mathrm{FK}_k(q_t) = \bigl[p^k_t,\, r^k_t\bigr] \in \mathbb{R}^7,
\label{eq:fk7d}
\end{equation}
where $p^k_t \in \mathbb{R}^3$ is position and
$r^k_t \in \mathbb{R}^4$ orientation (unit quaternion) in the robot base
frame.

\textbf{Expert architecture.} The 4D Future Expert is a lightweight
transformer over temporal 4D tokens derived from $q_t$ and
$P^{\mathrm{hist}}_t$, plus conditioning from the VLM prefix. It outputs
reconstructed \textbf{current} 4D state $\hat{P}_t$, predicted
\textbf{future} trajectory $\hat{P}_{t+1:t+H}$, and hidden tokens
$Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t$ consumed by the World Knowledge Expert and Action
Expert.

\textbf{Role of $q_t$.} Proprioception enters both as raw input tokens
and as the quantity that defines FK targets. Conditioning on $q_t$
anchors predictions in the robot's instantaneous configuration; through
UBCA (Section~\ref{sec:wk}), $Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t$ also supplies metric
body context to the 2D foresight pathway.

\textbf{4D future loss:}
\begin{multline}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{4D}} = \beta \Bigl(
    \operatorname{MSE}\bigl(\hat{P}_t(q_t),\, P_t\bigr) \\
    + \gamma\, \operatorname{MSE}\bigl(\hat{P}_{t+1:t+H}(q_t),\, P_{t+1:t+H}\bigr)
\Bigr).
\label{eq:loss4d}
\end{multline}

\textbf{Current state loss:}
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cur}} = \operatorname{MSE}\bigl(\hat{p}_t,\, p_t\bigr).
\label{eq:losscur}
\end{equation}

\subsection{World Knowledge Expert and 4D--2D Alignment}
\label{sec:wk}

\textbf{Motivation.} Predicting future video alone does not guarantee
consistency with body motion. We introduce a compact \textbf{World
Knowledge Expert} that learns \textbf{implicit 2D-foresight tokens}
$Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times d}$, a low-dimensional
summary of what the camera should see, \textbf{aligned} with the 4D
Future Expert's predictions.

\textbf{Unified Bidirectional Cross-Attention (UBCA).} The 4D and World
Knowledge experts are two transformer modules, each carrying temporal
tokens. At layer $\ell$ and timestep $i$, for module
$A \in \{\mathrm{4D}, \mathrm{wk}\}$ with companion $B$:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{h}^A_{\ell,i} = \mathrm{Attn}\bigl(q(h^A_{\ell,i}),\; K^A_{\le i},\; V^A_{\le i}\bigr)
+ \mathrm{Attn}\bigl(q(h^A_{\ell,i}),\; K^B_{\le i},\; V^B_{\le i}\bigr),
\label{eq:ubca}
\end{equation}
with causal masking over timesteps: each token attends causally within
its stream and to causal history in the partner stream.
Fig.~\ref{fig:mask} shows the structured attention pattern.

\begin{figure}[t]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{ubca_mask.pdf}
    \caption{\textbf{UBCA attention structure.} The mask is shown at the
    block level for illustration. The VLM prefix applies causal
    self-attention. The 4D Future Expert and World Knowledge Expert use
    causal self-attention within their own streams and causal cross-attention
    to each other, forming the bidirectional UBCA pathway. Both streams
    attend to the VLM prefix, while the Action Expert uses full cross-attention
    to all three preceding streams and causal self-attention within its own
    stream. Colors denote causal self-attention, full cross-attention, causal
    UBCA, and masked connections.}
    \label{fig:mask}
\end{figure}

The world-knowledge pathway produces
\begin{equation}
Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t = f_{\mathrm{wk}}\bigl(o_t,\, \ell_t,\, q_t,\, Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:wk}
\end{equation}

During training, $Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t$ conditions a \textbf{frozen} video
diffusion transformer $g_{\mathrm{wm}}$. With VAE latents $x_0$, noise
$\epsilon$, and $\tau \sim \mathcal{U}[0,1]$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{vid}} = \mathbb{E}_{\tau,\epsilon}\Bigl[
    \bigl\| g_{\mathrm{wm}}(x_\tau,\, h(Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t)) - (\epsilon - x_0) \bigr\|^2
\Bigr].
\label{eq:lossvid}
\end{equation}

Only $f_{\mathrm{wk}}$, UBCA parameters, and projection $h$ are updated;
$g_{\mathrm{wm}}$ is \textbf{frozen}.

\subsection{Action Expert}
\label{sec:action}

The Action Expert generates action chunk $A_t$ via flow matching,
conditioned on the VLM prefix, $Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t$,
$Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t$, and $q_t$. With ground-truth $A^0_t$, noise
$\epsilon$, and $\tau \sim \mathcal{U}[0,1]$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{act}} = \mathbb{E}_{\tau,\epsilon}\Bigl[
    \bigl\| v_\theta(A^\tau_t,\, o_t,\, \ell_t,\, q_t,\, Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t,\, Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t) - (\epsilon - A^0_t) \bigr\|^2
\Bigr].
\label{eq:lossact}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Multi-Task Training Objective}
\label{sec:training}

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = \lambda_{\mathrm{cur}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cur}}
    + \lambda_{\mathrm{4D}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{4D}}
    + \lambda_{\mathrm{vid}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{vid}}
    + \lambda_{\mathrm{act}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{act}}
    + \lambda_{\mathrm{vqa}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{vqa}}.
\label{eq:losstotal}
\end{equation}

Post-training runs for \textbf{10{,}000} steps. The video world model
$g_{\mathrm{wm}}$ is \textbf{frozen}. All other trainable modules (VLM,
4D Future Expert, World Knowledge Expert, UBCA, projection $h$, and
Action Expert) are updated jointly from step~0. Let $\eta$ denote the
base learning rate for experts and the action head; the VLM uses
$\eta/5$.

\subsection{Inference}
\label{sec:inference}

At deployment: (1)~encode $(o_t, \ell_t)$ with the VLM; (2)~run both
experts with UBCA to obtain $Z^{\mathrm{4D}}_t$, $Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t$;
(3)~run the Action Expert to output $A_t$; (4)~\textbf{do not} invoke
$g_{\mathrm{wm}}$. The extra cost vs.\ a plain 2D VLA is two lightweight
expert forward passes and FK on $q_t$.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate 4DW-VLA on two simulation benchmarks
(RoboTwin~2.0~\cite{chen2025robotwin2} and LIBERO-Plus) and on a
real-robot platform. We ask: (1)~Does the 4D Future Expert improve
success over VLAs that lack explicit 3D/4D body motion?
(2)~Does the same recipe improve robustness under domain randomization?
(3)~Do gains generalize across benchmarks and transfer to real hardware?

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\textbf{Benchmark.} RoboTwin~2.0 is a dual-arm manipulation suite on
SAPIEN with domain randomization over clutter, lighting, background
texture, table height, and language~\cite{chen2025robotwin2}. Easy =
\texttt{demo\_clean}; Hard = \texttt{demo\_randomized} (OOD). Evaluations
use the Aloha-AgileX bimanual embodiment; success rate is the metric.

\textbf{Tasks.} \texttt{stack\_bowls\_three} (geometrically precise
stacking), \texttt{scan\_object} (dual-arm tool use), and
\texttt{place\_bread\_skillet} (distinct placement objective).

\textbf{Baselines.} We compare against two published VLAs that represent
complementary design points; neither serves as an architectural parent of
4DW-VLA.

\textbf{InternVLA-A1.5}~\cite{internvla15} represents the
\textbf{2D-foresight VLA} family: temporal reasoning and future
\textbf{2D video} distillation, but \textbf{no explicit 3D/4D body
motion} or 4D--2D alignment. We re-implement and train it under the same
post-training budget as 4DW-VLA.

\textbf{LingBot-VLA~2.0}~\cite{lingbot2026} represents the
\textbf{depth-plus-video VLA} family: \textbf{3D (depth)} cues and
temporal video generation, but \textbf{no coupling} of 3D/4D body
trajectories with 2D temporal evolution. We do not reproduce
LingBot-VLA~2.0; numbers are \textbf{as-reported}. Not evaluated on
\texttt{scan\_object} or \texttt{place\_bread\_skillet} (n/a).

\textbf{Training recipe.} 4DW-VLA and other methods
share a \textbf{10{,}000-step} post-training budget. The video world
model $g_{\mathrm{wm}}$ is \textbf{frozen}. Expert and action-head
parameters use base learning rate $\eta$; the VLM uses $\eta/5$
(Table~\ref{tab:recipe}).

\textbf{\texttt{stack\_bowls\_three}.} 4DW-VLA reaches 81\%/58\%

\subsection{Discussion}

Easy scores match or exceed other methods on all three RoboTwin~2.0
tasks, indicating that 4D and aligned 2D auxiliary heads need not hurt
in-domain learning. Consistent Hard improvements support the hypothesis
that \textbf{explicit 4D--2D coupling} matters more under domain shift
than \textbf{2D video foresight alone}.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Body-centric 4D, not full scene geometry.} FK trajectories
  capture the robot's own motion, not independent object dynamics or
  extrinsic scene structure.
\item \textbf{FK accuracy depends on calibration.} URDF--hardware
  mismatch propagates into 4D labels; sim-to-real kinematics gap is
  unstudied.
\item \textbf{World-model distillation quality.} Removing
  $g_{\mathrm{wm}}$ at inference assumes $Z^{\mathrm{wk}}_t$ captures
  sufficient visual dynamics.
\item \textbf{UBCA ablations pending.} Late concatenation and
  unidirectional cross-attention are not yet compared.
\item \textbf{Limited task diversity.} Evaluation covers RoboTwin~2.0 and
  LIBERO-Plus; broader benchmarks and object categories remain untested.
\end{itemize}

% ====================================================================
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{4DW-VLA}, a \textbf{4D and world-model aligned VLA}
for robotic manipulation. Its central component is a \textbf{4D Future
Expert} that derives lightweight 7D body trajectories from FK and is
\textbf{retained at deployment}, unlike prior FK-based approaches that
discard the 4D branch after training. Coupled with a World Knowledge
Expert through bidirectional cross-attention and a frozen video world
model used only during training, the architecture produces a small,
fast policy with 4D-aligned visual foresight and no deployment-time video
generation. On RoboTwin~2.0 and LIBERO-Plus, 4DW-VLA matches or exceeds baselines
that lack explicit 4D body motion, with the strongest advantage under
domain randomization; real-robot experiments confirm sim-to-real transfer
of the FK-derived 4D signal. Future work will ablate the 4D expert
(retained vs.\ discarded, 7D vs.\ 3D) and extend to broader benchmarks.
